# [PATCH v7 04/15] zram: move mark_slot_accessed() under existing slot lock sections

来源：[https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261025021839.1542805-5-senozhatsky@chromium.org/](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261025021839.1542805-5-senozhatsky@chromium.org/)

```
zram_bio_read() and zram_bio_write() take slot lock just to
mark_slot_accessed(), right after zram_read_page() and
zram_write_page() have already taken and released it.  Move
mark_slot_accessed() into those existing slot lock read/write
sections.

Signed-off-by: Sergey Senozhatsky <senozhatsky@chromium.org>
```
