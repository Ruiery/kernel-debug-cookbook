# [PATCH 6/7] zsmalloc: fold zs_obj_write() into zs_store()

来源：[https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261105044752.1997895-7-senozhatsky@chromium.org/](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261105044752.1997895-7-senozhatsky@chromium.org/)

```
zram and zswap always call zs_obj_write() right after zs_malloc(), and
zs_obj_write() has to take pool->lock and look up object location
again, which zs_malloc() just did.

Replace zs_malloc() and zs_obj_write() with one single zs_store()
call.

Signed-off-by: Sergey Senozhatsky <senozhatsky@chromium.org>
```
