# [PATCH 42/98] block: partitions/mac: compute the sectors in 64 bits

来源：[https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261028164415.1262013-43-axboe@kernel.dk/](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261028164415.1262013-43-axboe@kernel.dk/)

```
From: Josef Bacik <josef@toxicpanda.com>

start_block and block_count are converted to 512 byte sectors in 32-bit
arithmetic, although put_partition() takes a sector_t. With a block size
above 512 bytes anything beyond 2TiB wraps and the partition lands on
top of another one.

Fixes: 1da177e4c3f4 ("Linux-2.6.12-rc2")
Signed-off-by: Josef Bacik <josef@toxicpanda.com>
Signed-off-by: Jens Axboe <axboe@kernel.dk>
```
