# [PATCH 01/13] bdev: Drop pointless loop from blkdev_flush_mapping()

来源：[https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261113084700.739002-14-jack@suse.cz/](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20261113084700.739002-14-jack@suse.cz/)

```
bdev_write_inode() is really odd loop calling write_inode_now(). The
bdev doesn't have any pages by now and if there are any dirty bits in
the inode state, they are stale. Just call write_inode_now() once to
clear them.

Signed-off-by: Jan Kara <jack@suse.cz>
```
