# [PATCH 4/7] drbd: raise the maximum resync rate to 8 GiB/s

来源：[https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20260923140608.1116713-5-christoph.boehmwalder@linbit.com/](https://lore.kernel.org/linux-block/20260923140608.1116713-5-christoph.boehmwalder@linbit.com/)

```
The resync-rate limit tops out at 4 GiB/s, which was chosen for bonded
10 GbE. Current hardware exceeds that with a single link. Double the
limit to 8 GiB/s, matching DRBD 9, and make the constant unsigned like
its neighbours.

Signed-off-by: Christoph Böhmwalder <christoph.boehmwalder@linbit.com>
```
